## Historical batch reports

Each numbered batch records the commands, inputs and observations from that run.
A passing historical batch does not certify a later commit, fixture distribution,
external engine or previously unexecuted workflow. Retired fixture paths and pin
values in old reports describe those old inputs; they are not current setup
instructions. Code-reference names removed during the authorised history cleanup
do not change native outcome counts or turn missing coverage into completed work.

Use [testing.md](../docs/testing.md) for current grouped fixture-ID lookup,
candidate/released pins and no-write checks. New reports keep scoped and full/race
results separate. Generated reports under `reports/acceptance/` are local outputs;
committed snapshots retain their original result scope.
